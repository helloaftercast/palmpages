Questions

You built a site without asking?

Yes — a draft, from what is already public: your menu or tours, your photos, your Google reviews. It is easier to show you than to describe it. Nothing is published anywhere until you say so, and if you would rather we delete it, say the word and it's gone.

How do orders and bookings reach me?

A guest taps through the page, picks dishes or a tour, and the site opens WhatsApp with the message written out. You confirm it like any other chat. There is no app for you to learn and nothing for the guest to install.

There is no online payment on the site. Guests pay you the way they do today. We stay out of the money.

Do you take a percentage of my bookings?

No. You pay once for the site and that is the end of it. Orders and bookings go straight from the guest to your phone; we are not in the middle and we never see your money.

Do you do SEO? Will ChatGPT recommend me?

The page has a real URL, real text, hours, and markup so Google — and other systems that read the open web — can see who you are, where you are, and how to book. That is the part we control.

We do not sell a promise that ChatGPT, Gemini or Google AI will name you in two weeks. Rankings also depend on reviews, links, and who already sits on your search. If someone guarantees that, they are selling the slogan.

I run kayak or surf trips, not a restaurant.

Same product. Price, duration, meeting point, what to bring, a weather note, and WhatsApp to book. No GetYourGuide calendar and no third-party booking API — those are a different job.

What if I only want a simple page, no ordering?

That is a common choice. Photos, prices, reviews, hours and a WhatsApp button — the booking stays a conversation, exactly as it works today.

Who changes prices when they go up?

We do. Send a message with what changed and it is usually updated within a day or two. Two rounds are included in the first year; after that it is a small flat fee per batch, not per item.

My customers use Viber, Zalo or LINE, not WhatsApp.

The button can point at whichever one you actually answer. WhatsApp is the smoothest: the message arrives fully written out. LINE, Zalo and Viber open the chat but cannot pre-fill it, so the site copies the text and the guest pastes — one extra tap.

What happens to the site if you disappear?

You keep it. The domain is registered in your name and we hand over the files, so any developer can pick it up. That is the point of paying once instead of renting.
