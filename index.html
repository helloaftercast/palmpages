Questions

You built a site for my restaurant without asking?

Yes — a draft, from what is already public: your menu, your photos, your Google reviews. It is easier to show you than to describe it. Nothing is published anywhere until you say so, and if you would rather we delete it, say the word and it's gone.

How do the orders actually reach me?

A guest taps through your menu, picks their options, and the site opens WhatsApp with the whole order written out. You confirm it like any other chat. There is no app for you to learn, no tablet to watch, and nothing for the guest to install or sign up for.

There is no online payment on the site. Guests pay you the way they do today — at the table, on delivery, or by transfer if you ask them in the chat. We stay out of the money.

Do you take a percentage of my orders?

No. You pay once for the site and that is the end of it. Orders go straight from the guest to your phone; we are not in the middle and we never see your money.

What if I only want a simple page, no ordering?

That is a common choice. We can build it as photos, menu, reviews, hours and a WhatsApp button — ordering stays a conversation, exactly as it works today.

Who changes the menu when prices go up?

We do. Send a message with what changed — prices, dishes, photos — and it is usually updated within a day or two. Two rounds are included in the first year; after that it is a small flat fee per batch of changes, not per item.

My customers use Viber, Zalo or LINE, not WhatsApp.

The ordering button can point at whichever one your restaurant actually answers. WhatsApp is the smoothest: the order arrives fully written out. LINE, Zalo and Viber open the chat but cannot pre-fill the message, so the site copies the order and the guest pastes it — one extra tap. Tell us which app your team lives in and we wire the site to that.

What happens to the site if you disappear?

You keep it. The domain is registered in your name and we hand over the files, so any developer can pick it up. That is the point of paying once instead of renting.
