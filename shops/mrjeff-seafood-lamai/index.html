Roadside shack energy — ice chest out front, grill in the back, Mr. Jeff at the door.

  1. Pick the catch

    King lobster (the famous one), prawns, fish, mussels, crab — priced when the kitchen weighs it. Prices range from ฿200 to ฿1,200 per dish, depending on the catch of the day.

  2. Say the fire

    Grilled, fried, BBQ, steamed, or curry powder sauce — the crab curry powder is a house favourite.

  3. Add Thai sides

    Pad Thai, seaweed salad, fried rice — ask Mr. Jeff for today’s price.

  4. Pay cash

    Send the order ahead on LINE or WhatsApp; weight and price confirmed by the kitchen. Cash only at the till.
