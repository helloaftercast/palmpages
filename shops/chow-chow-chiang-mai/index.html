About

A contemporary Thai kitchen

A new Thai contemporary kitchen with a Southeast Asian inspired twist. The room is on Charoen Prathet Road in Chang Khlan, opposite Hotel Meliá.

The kitchen

The paper menu writes each plate the way they cook it: pomelo with a house yum, scallop ceviche with coconut cream, crispy pork that takes as long as a long film. The house lists itself as LGBTQ+ friendly.

Where it sits

17/4 Charoen Prathet Road, Chang Khlan, Mueang Chiang Mai, Chiang Mai 50100. Lunch and dinner, dark on Monday.

Events

The table fills

There is no public events list. The dining room is small. Guests who arrived without a booking have written about a wait of about twenty minutes, and a drink while they waited. For a table, or a sitting for a group, call the number on the door.
