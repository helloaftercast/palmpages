About

A contemporary Thai kitchen

A new Thai contemporary kitchen with a Southeast Asian inspired twist. Charoen Prathet Road, Chang Khlan, opposite Hotel Meliá.

The kitchen

Pomelo with the house yum, scallop with coconut cream, crispy pork that takes as long as a long film. LGBTQ+ friendly.

Where it sits

17/4 Charoen Prathet Road, Chang Khlan, Mueang Chiang Mai 50100. Lunch and dinner. Closed Monday.
