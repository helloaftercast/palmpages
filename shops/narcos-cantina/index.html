How a night goes

Come at six for dinner or at midnight after the bars. Same kitchen, same music.

  1. Tacos

    Chicken, pork, fish and vegetarian at ₱130. Beef ₱140, blackened shrimp ₱160. Six-inch handmade tortillas — start with two.

  2. Bigger

    Quesadillas, chicken nachos, burritos, enchiladas, chimichangas. Fried plantains arrive on the house.

  3. Drinks

    El Chapo's margarita, mojitos, buckets of San Miguel, and the house shot — Mauricio's Boom Boom, five for ₱300.

  4. Late

    Latin playlist, disco lights, live band nights. The doors don't close until 5 a.m.

Vegan and gluten-free friendly — corn tortillas and jackfruit tacos on request. GCash and PayMaya accepted.
