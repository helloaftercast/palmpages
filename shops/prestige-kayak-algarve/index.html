How the tour goes

Taken from their Airbnb listing — same order they wrote. Beginner, light activity. Portuguese, English or Spanish.

  1. Meet at Praia Nova

    The guide meets you, gives a safety briefing and how to paddle, then hands out life jackets.

  2. Along the coast

    A relaxed departure. They stay on paddling and safety before the first cave.

  3. Caves and photo stops

    Into caves they can enter that day, and stops at the rock. They write that they avoid the busiest stretches.

  4. Captains' Cave

    The high point on their own list: the formations, and what they know of the place. Then a quiet return to Praia Nova.
