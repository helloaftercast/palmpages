How an evening goes

Come after the rocks, or stay for a slow dinner when the breeze picks up upstairs.

  1. Arrive

    Soi Hin Ta & Hin Yai in Maret — a few minutes from Grandfather & Grandmother Rocks on Lamai Beach.

  2. Order
