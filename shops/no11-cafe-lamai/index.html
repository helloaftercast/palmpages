How a day at No.11 goes

Morning eggs, a walk toward the rocks, Thai lunch, then a drink before we close at six.

  1. Brunch

    English breakfast, Italian baked eggs, club sandwich — doors open at 09:00.

  2. Beach breeze

    We sit near Hin Ta Hin Yai and the Dune strip. Stretch your legs toward the sand between courses.

  3. Thai lunch

    Chicken curry, pad Thai, pancakes or parfait — confirm lunch prices in chat.

  4. Cocktails before 6

    Mojito, piña colada, cappuccino or smoothie. We close at 18:00 — Mondays we’re off.
