How a beach day goes

Arrive for coffee, stay through lunch. Order something — sunbeds and towels are free with food or drink.

  1. Arrive

    Find us on Had Lamai Road, right on the sand. Open every day from 08:30.

  2. Claim a bed

    Free sunbeds and towels when you order. Settle in under the palms facing the gulf.

  3. Breakfast

    Eggs, omelets, croissants, Big Breakfast — French-run kitchen, Thai morning light.

  4. Lunch & leave

    Shrimp noodles, tartare, massaman, pad Thai. We close at 18:00 — no late night rush.
