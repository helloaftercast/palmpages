How the day trip goes

From their own day-trip page. Morning only. Pickup 7:15–7:30. Back around 16:30. Suitable for first-timers.

  1. Hotel pickup

    Van to Bangrong Pier. Your guide is there. Speedboat ferry to Koh Yao Noi, about 30 minutes.

  2. Longtail to the first island

    About 30–40 minutes on a private longtail. They usually start at Koh Kudu Yai, then Kudu Noi.

  3. Hong at Koh Roi

    A short sea cave into a hong — a hidden inner lagoon. Time permitting, Koh Batang. The guide can change the line.

  4. Lunch, then the 16:00 ferry

    Thai dishes from a family restaurant on Koh Yao Noi, in reusable boxes. 16:00 speedboat back to Bangrong, then the van.
