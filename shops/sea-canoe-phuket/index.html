How a Phang Nga day goes

From their self-paddle itinerary. Pickup 08:30–08:50. Ao Por Pier 09:15. Back to the pier around 16:00, then the hotel.

  1. Hotel pickup

    Van from your hotel on Phuket, 08:30–08:50. Ao Por Pier on the northeast of the island at 09:15.

  2. Escort boat into the bay

    Leave Ao Por around 09:30 on the escort boat or a fast longtail. Beginners get kayak instruction on the way.

  3. Caves and hidden lagoons

    Around 10:30 in the bay. You paddle sit-on-top kayaks into caves and hongs — including Panak Island on the self-paddle days.

  4. Lunch, then the last island

    Seafood buffet lunch on the water. A third stop along the limestone coast. Return to Ao Por around 16:00, then drop-off at the hotel.
