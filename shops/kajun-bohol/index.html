How it works

One secret spice blend from Ankara, one fryer, no shortcuts.

  1. Pick your bird

    Tenders or wings — Kajun, hot, or buffalo. Six or ten pieces, with fries, rice or mash.

  2. Or a burger

    Fried-chicken towers, 160g beef patties, or the crispy chicken sandwich.

  3. Round it out

    Fresh-fruit shakes, lemonades, French-press coffee. Waffles and flan after.

  4. Cool down

    Freeze-cold AC inside, seats outside. 100% halal — no pork, no alcohol. Cards accepted.
