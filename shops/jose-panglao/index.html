How paluto works

The old market way: you choose the fish, you choose the fire.

  1. Pick your catch

    Pompano, tuna belly, squid, crab — on ice by the door. Grouper still swimming.

  2. Say the word

    Grilled over coals, sinigang, sweet & sour, steamed, tinola, or kinilaw — raw, cured in vinegar.

  3. Priced by weight

    Everything by the hundred grams. Half a kilo feeds two; a kilo feeds the table.

  4. Eat under the trees

    Open-air nipa hut, buckets of cold beer, live singers some nights. Come as you are.

Open every day, 11:00 – 22:30. Dine in or take out.
