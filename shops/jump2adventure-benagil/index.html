    Before you come

    I have never paddled.

    Most people here have not. Ten minutes on the sand, then we go. If someone is not happy in the first minutes, we turn around.

    What if the weather turns?

    We look at wind and swell. Bad morning: afternoon, or another day. We do not launch to prove a booking.

    Do I need to book?

    Yes. Walk-ups get leftover seats, if any. Since 2024 you need a guide on that stretch of coast.

    Why book here and not on a platform?

    Same kayaks, same van. The platforms keep about 20–25% of what you pay. This page writes to us.

    Do I pay on the site?

    No. You pay us the way you already do — on the day, or however we agree in the chat.
