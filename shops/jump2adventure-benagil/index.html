How the tour goes

About two hours. A guide stays with you the whole time. Benagil cave, up to three smaller caves if the sea allows, then a stop at Baía dos Arcos da Marinha.

  1. Meet and kit

    Car park opposite Restaurante O Litoral, Lagoa. Arrive 30 minutes early. Life vest, paddle, dry bag, a short briefing.

  2. Into the water

    The guide puts each person in. Most people here have never paddled. Ten minutes on the sand, then we go.

  3. Benagil cave

    The known cave. A short stop for a look, a few facts, and photographs. You stay with the guide.

  4. Smaller caves, then the arches

    Up to three smaller caves, only if the sea allows. A short stop at Baía dos Arcos da Marinha. Then the same line back.

    Before you come

    I have never paddled.

    Most people here have not. Ten minutes on the sand, then we go. If someone is not happy in the first minutes, we turn around.

    What if the weather turns?

    We look at wind and swell. Bad morning: afternoon, or another day. We do not launch to prove a booking.

    What do we actually visit?

    Benagil cave. Up to three smaller caves if the sea allows. A short stop at Baía dos Arcos da Marinha. Then back to the same car park.

    When should I arrive?

    Thirty minutes before the slot. Briefing and kit happen before you launch.

    Which languages?

    Portuguese, English, Spanish, German and French.

    Do I need to book?

    Yes. Walk-ups get leftover seats, if any. Since 2024 you need a guide on that stretch of coast.

    Why book here and not on a platform?

    Same kayaks, same guides. The platforms keep about 20–25% of what you pay. This page writes to us.

    Do I pay on the site?

    No. You pay us the way you already do — on the day, or however we agree in the chat.
