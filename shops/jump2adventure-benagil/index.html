    Before you paddle

    Do I need to book?

    Yes. Since 2024 you cannot paddle the cave zone without a guide. Walk-ups get the leftover slots, if any.

    What if the weather turns?

    We look at wind and swell, not the forecast app on the hotel wifi. Bad morning: we move you to the afternoon or another day. We do not send you out to prove a booking.

    Can I swim in the cave?

    When the sea is quiet, we stop. When it isn't, we don't. That call is the guide's, on the water.

    Why not GetYourGuide?

    Same kayaks, same van. They keep about 20–25% of what you pay. This page sends the booking to us.

    Do I pay on the site?

    No. You pay us the way you already do — on the day, or however we agree in the chat.
