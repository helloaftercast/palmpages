How a day at Doña Yola goes

Late breakfast, shade under the trees, home plates cooked as you sit, then a margarita before we close at 19:30.

  1. Chilaquiles

    Doors open at 11:00. Eggs, salsa, and clay plates — the Hotel Zone breakfast guests come back for.

  2. Under the trees

    Look for the turquoise chairs under the trees, behind the pharmacy — that’s us, next to Plaza Caracol.

  3. Home plates

    Shrimp fajitas, arrachera burrito, quesadillas — the aunts cook each plate as you sit.

  4. Margaritas before 7:30

    Piña coladas, papaya smoothie, café de olla. Last seating before 19:30 — we’re closed Sunday and Monday.
