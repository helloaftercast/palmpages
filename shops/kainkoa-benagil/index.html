How the tour goes

From their own tour copy and from the Marina TicketShop page that sells this outing. Two hours. Portuguese, English or Spanish.

  1. Meet on Benagil beach

    Praia de Benagil, Estr. de Benagil. Arrive 30 minutes early. Guide, kayak, life vest.

  2. Sunrise or sunset launch

    They sell this slot as the quieter hour inside the cave — not the middle of the day boat crush.

  3. Benagil cave

    They write that you can get out of the kayak, stand on the sand, and take photographs. A boat does not stop like that.

  4. End at the Marinha arches

    Neighbouring beaches and other caves if the sea allows, then the arches of Praia da Marinha.

    Why sunrise or sunset?

    That is the product they sell: a small group, away from the usual crush inside the cave.

    They say yes — step on the sand and take photographs. Sea and the day's rules decide.

    What if the weather turns?

    Marina TicketShop writes that the tour is always subject to the weather.

    Do I pay on the site?

    No. You pay them the way you already do — on the day, or however you agree in the chat.
